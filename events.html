2025–26 recap

Last year at HPF

Welcome Brunch

We welcomed the HPF community and new freshmen to campus with our semesterly welcome brunches.

FILC at UPenn

We traveled to UPenn for the 2026 Filipino Ivy League Conference to connect with the other clubs.

Cultural Rhythms

We performed Tinikling and Sayaw sa Bangko at the 2026 Cultural Rhythms Showcase.

Senior Send-Off

We celebrated our graduating seniors and J-Term alumni with food and parting gifts.

Class of 2026

Congratulations to our graduates!

Our seniors celebrated at the Filipino Graduation Ceremony, including HPF presidents Aaron Arlanza, Laurinne Eugenio, and Jayden Acoba. Thank you for being part of the Harvard HPF Pamilya!
