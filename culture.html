Culture

Dance, food, and pamilya

HPF is asked to perform at several cultural shows every year, most notably Cultural Rhythms, Harvard's premier event showcasing the diversity at the college; Kamayan, District One's annual cultural show; and Southeast Asia Night, an evening at Harvard celebrating Southeast Asian culture through food and performances.

Recent performance

Cultural Rhythms 2026

This spring we performed Tinikling and Sayaw sa Bangko at the 2026 Cultural Rhythms Showcase. Tinikling has dancers stepping between clapping bamboo poles, and Sayaw sa Bangko is performed on top of a narrow wooden bench.

On campus

Brunches and study breaks

At the beginning of each semester, HPF hosts a welcome brunch with spam, eggs, and rice to welcome new members to the Pamilya and for current members to catch up with others.

HPF also holds study breaks throughout the year featuring delicious Filipino food and snacks, games, and more fun!
