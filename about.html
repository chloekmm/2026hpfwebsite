About

What is HPF?

The Harvard Philippine Forum (HPF) is a close-knit family of Filipinos, Filipino-Americans, and friends that celebrates and shares the vitality of Philippine culture and tradition with the Harvard community.

A uniquely Filipino Harvard experience

Through activities from traditional dance performances to food samplings, HPF offers the campus community a glimpse of Filipino culture. With its social and educational events and the camaraderie it offers, it provides its members with a uniquely Filipino Harvard experience.

HPF's activities fall into three main categories: culture, education, and service.

Who can join?

Absolutely anyone!

We invite everyone and anyone to join our pamilya (family)! You don't need to be Filipino to be part of HPF, just curious and ready for good food and good company.

Our network

FIND, Inc. and District One

Founded in 1992, the Filipino Intercollegiate Networking Dialogue, Inc. (FIND, Inc.) is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting unity and cooperation among Filipino and Filipino-American students along the East Coast. FIND, Inc. serves over 3,000 students in East Coast colleges, universities, vocational, and technical schools, divided into seven districts by geography.

District One (D1) of FIND, Inc. represents schools in Eastern New England. It is dedicated to promoting awareness of issues pertaining to Filipinos nationwide, preserving and promoting Filipino culture, and providing the means for initiating action and dialogue within the community.

Learn more at findinc.org, follow D1 on Facebook, or sign up for the D1 newsletter.
