Education

Speaker events

HPF invites speakers to campus every year to speak to students about pertinent Filipino and Filipino-American issues, and to promote dialogue and open discussion about the issues Filipinos face.

Past speakers

  • Leni RobredoFormer Vice President of the Philippines, in partnership with Wellesley Club Kapatid and the Harvard University Asia Center
  • Prof. Alex OrquizaHistory professor at Providence College and author of Taste of Control: Food and the Filipino Colonial Mentality Under American Rule
  • Justine RamosAuthor of Halo-Halo: A poetic mix of culture, history, identity, revelation, and revolution
  • Mark SanchezHarvard University History & Literature instructor
  • Jia TolentinoStaff writer at The New Yorker
  • Anna Marie CruzFounder of Entrepinayship
